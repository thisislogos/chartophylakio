Insurance Plans <<Page>>
Wherever you’re going, You Go Insurance is there for you. 
Whether you’re relocating overseas, working remotely, travelling for business or taking a well-earned getaway, our flexible international health and travel insurance helps you stay protected wherever life takes you. 
From everyday healthcare to the unexpected moments that matter, we’re here when you need us, with cover designed around your priorities, your destination, and the way you live and travel.
Global Expat Plan
Moving abroad for a year or longer? 
The Global Expat Plan is your all-inclusive, long-term health partner for Americans abroad or those needing cover in the US. 
Designed for global professionals, and families settling overseas, this plan offers complete private medical coverage worldwide—including full access to top-tier care and hospitals in the United States. 
Enjoy preventive wellness checks, specialized inpatient/outpatient treatments, and routine care without borders.
Global Expat Plan without US Cover 
Heading overseas long-term and don't need coverage in the United States? 
The Global Expat No US Cover Plan provides the same robust, long-term private health insurance as our signature Expat coverage, fully valid anywhere in the world except the US. 
By tailoring your geographical zone, you unlock significant savings while securing world-class private healthcare for stays of 1 year or longer.
Global Visitor Plan
Planning a trip for less than a year, cross-border business trip, or semester abroad? 
The Global Visitor Plan offers dynamic travel insurance built for short trips less than one year. It protects your wallet against sudden travel mishaps—from lost luggage and delayed flights to acute emergency medical care while you’re far from home.
