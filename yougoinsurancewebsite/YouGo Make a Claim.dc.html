Emergency Assistance

Emergency situations

If you have a medical emergency, seek treatment immediately. Our emergency assistance line is available 24/7:

+1 (800) 997 7341 Free via Viber

Our emergency team can help with:

Informing the medical facility of your cover
Arranging direct payment to the medical facility
Arranging or pre-approving treatment
Organizing an emergency medical evacuation

If you are hospitalized

Within 48 hours

If you are admitted to hospital, you, your doctor, a dependent, or a colleague must contact us within 48 hours to let us know about the hospitalization.

Keep us informed

You should also contact your dedicated team at hello@yougoinsurance.com to keep us informed.

Have this ready

Always have your policy number when contacting us about an emergency or claim.

Non-Emergency Situations

For non-emergency questions or support, contact your dedicated team. We can help with:

Signing up for a policy
Help with a claim
Arranging payment with a digital card
Policy pricing and payments
Providing proof of insurance
Checking your benefits and limits
Making changes to your policy
Any other questions about your cover
Email hello@yougoinsurance.com
Phone, WhatsApp or Viber +60 (11) 1051 2677
Opening hours Monday to Friday, 10am–6pm (GMT+8)

Useful Contacts

Claims submissions claims@yougoinsurance.com
Pre-approval pre@yougoinsurance.com
Medical emergency evacuations +1 (800) 997 7341

Remember: You will need your policy number when making a claim or contacting us about your cover.
