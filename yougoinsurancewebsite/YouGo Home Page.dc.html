Key Differences

International Health Insurance or Travel Insurance?

Purpose

Travel insurance covers unexpected mishaps on a short trip. Health insurance covers ongoing, everyday healthcare needs.

Medical Care

Travel insurance only pays for sudden emergencies or accidents abroad. Health insurance covers routine doctor visits, prescriptions, and specialist care.

Non-Medical Perks

Travel insurance covers trip cancellations, delays, and lost luggage. Health insurance does not cover travel disruptions or personal items.

Duration

Travel insurance lasts for a single trip or a few months. Health insurance is a continuous, year-round policy.

Cost

Because Travel insurance only pays for sudden emergencies or accidents abroad, and not the on-going treatment after you are returned to your home country, it is usually cheaper. Health insurance covers long-term treatment as well as routine doctor visits, prescriptions, and specialist care.
